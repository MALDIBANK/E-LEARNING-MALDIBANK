# Final Exam

You have reached the end of the course. The final exam checks what you learned in the five modules: safety and materials, sample preparation for yeast, filamentous fungi and bacteria, preparing the MALDI-TOF target plate, and cleaning reusable target plates.

## Before you start

- Complete all five modules first.
- The exam has 12 questions (true/false and multiple choice).
- You need at least 80% correct answers to pass.
- It takes about 10 minutes.
- You will be asked for your name and email address. Your result will be sent to the email address you provide.
