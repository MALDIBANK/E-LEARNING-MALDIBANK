# Sample Preparation for Bacteria

## Using solid culture media

### Culture

Culture the bacterial strains on the appropriate solid culture medium for 6 to 48h at optimal growth temperature. If growth is too limited after 48h, extend the incubation until sufficient material is available to perform an extraction.

### Protein extraction

1. In a 1.5 ml microtube, resuspend bacteria (from a single colony up to the size of a lentil) in 1 ml of ultrapure water.
2. Centrifuge at 13,000 g for 2 minutes and discard the supernatant.
3. Resuspend the pellet in 300 µl of ultrapure water and add 900 µl of ethanol. Mix gently by pipetting or vortexing.
4. Centrifuge for 2 minutes at 13,000 g.
5. Remove the supernatant by pipetting or decanting.
6. Centrifuge again briefly (1–2 minutes) at 13,000 g and remove the residual supernatant.
7. Let the pellet air-dry for approximately 5–15 minutes.
8. Once dried, add 40 µl of 70% formic acid and vortex gently (a compact pellet can be fragmented with the pipette tip while adding the acid).
9. Add 40 µl of acetonitrile and vortex gently.

> **Note:** you can vary the formic acid / acetonitrile volume between 10 and 80 µl, but the two volumes must always be equal. A very small pellet needs 10–20 µl; a large pellet can need up to 80 µl. The added volume should just cover the pellet.

10. Centrifuge for 2 minutes at 13,000 g. The supernatant is the final extract. Store at **-20°C** until further analysis.

## Using liquid culture media

### Culture

Inoculate the liquid cultivation tubes with the biological material and close the lid. Incubate for 7h to overnight at optimal growth temperature. If growth is slow, extend the incubation until sufficient material is available.

### Protein extraction

1. Transfer 1 ml of the liquid culture into a 1.5 ml microtube.
2. Centrifuge at 13,000 g for 2 minutes and discard the supernatant.
3. Resuspend the pellet in 1 ml of ultrapure water, centrifuge again at 13,000 g for 2 minutes, and discard the supernatant.
4. Resuspend the pellet in 300 µl of ultrapure water and add 900 µl of ethanol. Mix gently by pipetting or vortexing.
5. Centrifuge for 2 minutes at 13,000 g.
6. Remove the supernatant by pipetting or decanting.
7. Centrifuge again briefly (1–2 minutes) at 13,000 g and remove the residual supernatant.
8. Let the pellet air-dry for approximately 5–15 minutes.
9. Once dried, add 40 µl of 70% formic acid and vortex gently.
10. Add 40 µl of acetonitrile and vortex gently (same volume-adjustment rule as the solid-media protocol).
11. Centrifuge for 2 minutes at 13,000 g. The supernatant is the final extract. Store at **-20°C** until further analysis.
