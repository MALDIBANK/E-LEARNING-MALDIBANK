# Preparation of the MALDI-TOF Target Plate

1. Place 1 µl of the final extract onto a sample spot of the MALDI-TOF target plate. Foresee one spot with the BTS (calibration and positive control).
2. Let the extracts and BTS dry completely at room temperature. For faster drying, use an elevated temperature (35°C) or a manufacturer device such as the Bruker MBT FAST Shuttle.

> **Note:** for identification, analyse the extracts in replicates (2 to 4 spots per extract) to check for consistency of the identification results.

> **Note:** to create a sample reference spectrum, a minimum of 8 replicates is required, each shot 3 times, for a total minimum of 24 spectra.

3. Add 1 µl of HCCA matrix to overlay the spots with dried extracts or BTS. Foresee one spot with HCCA matrix only (no extract or BTS) as a negative control, to verify the matrix alone produces no peaks.
4. Let the HCCA matrix dry completely (same procedure as for the extract).

The plate is then ready to be analysed.

> **Note:** once a sample extract has dried, the HCCA matrix must be added within 30 minutes. Prepared MALDI-TOF target plates must be analysed within 24h.
