# Cleaning of Reusable MALDI-TOF Target Plates

> **Safety reminder:** work in a chemical fume hood — TFA is very corrosive.

1. Soak the plate in 70% ethanol for five minutes.
2. Rinse the plate with tap water and dry with a precision wipe.
3. Rinse the plate with acetone and dry with a precision wipe.
4. Rinse the plate with 70% ethanol and dry with a precision wipe.
5. Rinse the plate with tap water and dry with a precision wipe.
6. Place 100 µl of 80% TFA and spread it onto the entire surface of the plate with a pipette tip, loop, or wipe (rubbing the surface with the wipe).
7. Dry the plate with a precision wipe.
8. Rinse the plate with ultrapure water and dry with a precision wipe.
9. Let the plate dry completely for 10–20 minutes in the open air.

This step applies only if your target plates are reusable — disposable plates do not require this procedure.
