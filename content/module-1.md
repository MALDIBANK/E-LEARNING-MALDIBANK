# Microbial Identification by MALDI-TOF Mass Spectrometry: Sample Preparation

## Introduction

This procedure describes the sample preparation for the identification of bacteria, yeast, and filamentous fungi by matrix-assisted laser desorption/ionization – time-of-flight mass spectrometry (MALDI-TOF).

This course encompasses the standard operating procedure (SOP) for spectra acquisition for microbial identification using MALDI-TOF MS.

## Learning outcomes

At the end of this course, you will be able to:

- Identify and select the materials and reagents needed to prepare samples (bacteria, yeast, and filamentous fungi) for identification using MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry
- Prepare reagents needed for the assay
- Perform sample preparation for yeast and filamentous fungi using solid culture media
- Perform sample preparation for filamentous fungi using liquid media
- Perform sample preparation for bacteria using solid and liquid culture media
- Prepare the MALDI-TOF MS target plate
- If applicable: clean reusable MALDI-TOF target plates

## Abbreviations

- **BTS** — Bacterial Test Standard (Bruker)
- **HCCA** — α-cyano-4-hydroxy-cinnamic acid
- **MALDI-TOF MS** — Matrix Assisted Laser Desorption Ionization Time-Of-Flight Mass Spectrometry
- **TFA** — Trifluoroacetic acid

## Health & safety

- Wear a lab coat and gloves during the practical work.
- Work in a microbial biosafety cabinet (vertical laminar flow).
- For the cleaning of reusable MALDI-TOF target plates: work in a chemical fume hood — TFA is very corrosive.

## Equipment

- MALDI-TOF mass spectrometer (e.g. Bruker Sirius)
- MALDI-TOF MS target plate (if applicable, with adapter)
- Microbial biosafety cabinet
- Chemical fume hood
- Centrifuge
- Vortex
- Micropipettes

## Consumables

- Microtubes (1.5 ml, sterile). **Avoid tubes with surface treatment (e.g. silicone).**
- Falcon tubes (50 ml, sterile)
- Disposable Pasteur pipettes (sterile)
- Tips for micropipettes
- Plastic forceps or loops (sterile)
- Ethanol (absolute or analytical grade, non-denatured)
- Formic acid (analytical grade)
- Acetonitrile (analytical grade)
- HCCA matrix — prepared following manufacturer's instructions
- Calibration standard solution (e.g. Bruker BTS) — prepared following manufacturer's instructions
- Ultrapure (e.g. MilliQ) water (sterile)
- Trifluoroacetic acid (TFA)
- Acetone
- Precision wipes (for delicate tasks)

## Solutions to prepare

**70% ethanol solution**
Mix 35 ml of absolute ethanol and 15 ml of ultrapure water in a 50 ml Falcon tube.

**70% formic acid solution**
Place 15 ml of ultrapure water in a 50 ml Falcon tube and add 35 ml of formic acid. Mix gently.

**80% TFA solution**
Place 5 ml of ultrapure water in a 50 ml Falcon tube and add 20 ml of TFA. Mix gently.
